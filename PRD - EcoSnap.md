# **Product Requirements Document (PRD): EcoSnap**

**Product Name:** EcoSnap

**Version:** 1.0

**Date:** July 22, 2025

**Target Audience:** Health-conscious consumers in India who want to understand the ingredients in their food, cosmetic, and household products.

### **1\. Vision & Mission**

**Vision:** To empower consumers to make informed and healthy purchasing decisions by providing transparent, instant, and easy-to-understand analysis of product ingredients.

**Mission:** Verdant is a mobile application that uses AI to instantly analyze product ingredients from a photo, flag potential concerns, and suggest safer, readily available alternatives in the Indian market.

### **2\. Core Features & User Stories**

#### **Feature 2.1: Onboarding & Home Screen**

* **Description:** The initial experience for the user. The home screen serves as the main dashboard, showing past scans and providing the primary call-to-action.  
* **User Stories:**  
  * As a new user, I want a brief, skippable onboarding that explains the app's core function: Scan, Analyze, and Discover Alternatives.  
  * As a user, I want the home screen to display a reverse chronological list of my previously scanned products for quick access.  
  * As a user, I want each item in my history to show the product name, its image, and a top-level rating (e.g., "Good," "Caution," "Avoid").  
  * As a user, I want a prominent, centrally located camera button at the bottom of the screen to start a new scan.  
  * As a user, I want a search bar at the top of the home screen to manually look for products.

#### **Feature 2.2: Camera Scan & Image Analysis (The Core Loop)**

* **Description:** The primary user flow for analyzing a new product in hand.  
* **User Stories:**  
  * As a user, I want to tap the camera button to open a full-screen camera view.  
  * As a user, the camera interface should prompt me to first take a clear, well-lit picture of the **product's ingredient list**.  
  * As a user, after capturing the ingredients, the app should prompt me to take a second picture of the **front of the product** for identification.  
  * As a user, I want the app to show a loading/processing indicator while it performs OCR and analysis.  
  * As a user, I want the app to handle common issues like blurry images or poor lighting with clear error messages, prompting me to try again.

#### **Feature 2.3: AI-Powered Report & Summary**

* **Description:** The screen that displays the results of the product analysis. This is the core value proposition.  
* **User Stories:**  
  * As a user, after a successful scan, I want to see a clear, concise summary of the product's analysis.  
  * As a user, this report screen must display the product's name and image at the top.  
  * As a user, I want to see an overall "EcoSnap Score" (e.g., Good, Caution, Avoid) based on the analysis.  
  * As a user, I want a "Key Concerns" section that lists and explains any harmful, controversial, or banned ingredients found (e.g., "Contains Parabens: Potential endocrine disruptors.").  
  * As a user, I want to see a full list of all detected ingredients, with the concerning ones highlighted.  
  * As a user, I want the summary to be generated by AI, written in simple, easy-to-understand language, avoiding overly technical jargon.

#### **Feature 2.4: Alternative Product Suggestions**

* **Description:** Provides actionable solutions for users when a product has a poor rating.  
* **User Stories:**  
  * As a user, on the report screen for a product with a "Caution" or "Avoid" rating, I want to see a clearly visible button or section titled "Find Better Alternatives."  
  * As a user, tapping this button should take me to a new screen listing safer, highly-rated alternative products from the same category (e.g., if I scanned a shampoo, it should show me other shampoos).  
  * As a user, each alternative product listed must show its name, image, a brief reason why it's a better choice (e.g., "Paraben-Free"), and its EcoSnap Score.  
  * As a user, I want to see "Best Buy" links for each alternative product, directing me to major Indian e-commerce sites like **Amazon.in, Flipkart, Nykaa, or BigBasket**.  
  * As a user, all suggested alternatives must be verified as available for purchase in India.

#### **Feature 2.5: Manual Product Search**

* **Description:** Allows users to look up products without having them physically present.  
* **User Stories:**  
  * As a user, I want to be able to type a product's name into the search bar on the home screen.  
  * As a user, the app should fetch and display a list of matching products (with their images) from a product database as I type.  
  * As a user, I want to be able to select the correct product from the search results.  
  * As a user, upon selecting a product, the app should generate the same AI-powered report and offer alternatives, just as if I had scanned it.

### **3\. Non-Functional Requirements**

* **Platform:** iOS and Android, built from a single codebase using **Flutter**.  
* **Backend & Database:** A scalable backend (**Node.js** or **Python**) with **Firebase Firestore** for the database to store user profiles, scan history, and ingredient data.  
* **UI/UX Principles:**  
  * **Clean & Intuitive:** A minimalist interface that guides the user effortlessly.  
  * **Responsive:** The app must look and function perfectly on various screen sizes, from small phones to large tablets.  
  * **Fast:** Scan, analysis, and search results should appear within 3-5 seconds to keep the user engaged.  
* **API Integrations:**  
  * **AI Analysis:** **Google's Gemini API** for analyzing the ingredient list and generating the summary. The prompt should be engineered to be robust and provide structured output (JSON is preferred).  
  * **Product Database & E-commerce:** A product data API (e.g., from **DataCrops** or a similar provider) to fetch product details for manual search and find alternatives with buy links for the Indian market.  
* **Data & Localization:** The app's primary operational region is **India**. All product data, alternatives, and e-commerce links must be relevant to the Indian market.
